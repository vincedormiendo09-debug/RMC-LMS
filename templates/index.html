What is our System?

A unified cloud-based Learning and Classroom Management platform engineered specifically for Regis Marie College. It consolidates course syllabi, assignment submissions, grading ledgers, and live group messaging into a single responsive portal.

What is the Purpose?

To eliminate manual paperwork bottlenecks, automate grade scoring using standard GPA equivalents, track submission punctuality, and provide direct feedback channels between faculty and enrolled students.

Why We Built This?

To resolve common academic hurdles: missed assignment deadlines, lost physical handouts, delayed score computations, and fragmented communication between instructors and class groups.

What is our Goal?

To cultivate academic excellence, foster transparency in student evaluation, provide timely intervention for at-risk learners, and equip Regis Marie College with modern digital educational infrastructure.

User Navigation & Operations Guide

Follow the step-by-step workflow designed for your academic role inside the portal.

Student Path

Course enrollment & assignment turn-ins
  • 1
    Request Registration: Submit your student details and degree program through the registration page.
  • 2
    Check Email: Receive your confirmation notice containing your registered portal credentials.
  • 3
    Sign In: Access your account at /login.
  • 4
    Home Hub (Landpage): View active enrolled subjects, project groups, and academic notices.
  • 5
    Join Class / Group: Use access codes provided by instructors (e.g., RMC-8X2A9) to enroll.
  • 6
    Study & Turn In: Download lecture handouts, complete activities, and submit work before deadlines.
  • 7
    Track Grades & Chat: View scores, GPA equivalents, and collaborate with peers in real-time group messenger.

Teacher / Faculty Path

Curriculum delivery & grading workflow
  • 1
    Account Login: Sign in using authorized faculty credentials at /login.
  • 2
    Create Space: Set up new subject classes and collaborative study groups in Create Class/Group.
  • 3
    Share Code: Distribute unique class codes to students for instant enrollment.
  • 4
    Publish Materials: Upload lecture handouts, reference links, and post activities with deadlines and point limits.
  • 5
    Evaluate Work: Inspect student attachments in the submissions queue, assign scores, and leave feedback.
  • 6
    Monitor Analytics: Track semester progression and identify at-risk learners on the Class & Student Dashboards.

Administrator Path

Institutional oversight & master records
  • 1
    Admin Sign In: Secure authentication with administrative privileges at /login.
  • 2
    Master Grade Ledger: Inspect system-wide student rosters and export official grade records to CSV format.
  • 3
    Institutional Analytics: Access full performance graphs for active content, turn-in timeliness, and subject averages.
  • 4
    Broadcast Alerts: Send system-wide announcements and notifications to faculty and students.
  • 5
    Term Archival: Safely archive completed academic semesters into the institutional vault.
